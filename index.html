Account & Data Requests

Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Amaram Kottakkal Ayurveda account and associated personal data. Our support team will receive your request and an administrator will review and process it.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact our support team
    Call or message us using the contact details below. State that you want your AMARAM account deleted.
  2. Identify your account
    Provide the mobile number registered to your account and any additional information our team reasonably needs to verify that you own it. Do not send passwords or OTPs.
  3. Administrator review
    Our administrator will review the request and process the account deletion. We may contact you if clarification or verification is required.
Please note: Sending a request does not mean the account has been deleted immediately. We will process the request after the necessary account verification and review.

What happens to your data?

Account and personal data eligible for deletion will be handled as part of the administrator's review. Certain records may need to be retained where required by applicable law or for legitimate medical, financial, security, or dispute-resolution obligations. If data must be retained, it will be retained only as required for the applicable purpose.
